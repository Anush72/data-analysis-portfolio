# 📡 Telco Customer Churn Analysis

Welcome to my **Telco Customer Churn Analysis** project! 

This project explores customer churn in a telecommunications dataset using a dual approach—leveraging **SQL** for deep data exploration and business question answering, and **Power BI** for visual storytelling and interactive dashboard development.

---

## 🎯 Project Goals & Objectives

- 🔍 **Understand Churn Drivers:** Investigate why customers leave the business.
- ⚠️ **Identify High-Risk Groups:** Pinpoint customer segments with higher probabilities of churning.
- 💰 **Evaluate Customer Value:** Analyze customer lifetime value (CLTV) and revenue at risk.
- 📊 **Bridge SQL & BI:** Combine data exploration with executive-ready visualizations.

---

## 🖥️ Dashboard Structure & Pages

1. **Overview:** High-level metrics, total customers, and overall churn rates.
2. **Customer Profile:** Demographics, senior citizen status, partner/dependents, and gender distribution.
3. **Churn Analysis:** Detailed breakdown by contract type, tenure, internet service, and additional tech services.
4. **Customer Value:** Financial metrics, total revenue, and CLTV analysis.
5. **Risk × Value Matrix:** Segmenting customers by business value and churn risk.
6. **Findings & Recommendations:** Actionable business insights and strategic next steps.

---

## 📊 Key Findings

* **Demographics:** Senior citizens exhibit a higher churn rate compared to non-senior citizens.
* **Contracts & Tenure:** Month-to-month contract holders are significantly more likely to churn, whereas longer tenure and two-year contracts correlate with lower churn and higher stable revenue.
* **Add-on Services:** Customers lacking auxiliary services (Online Security, Online Backup, Device Protection, and Tech Support)—particularly month-to-month users—show much higher churn rates.
* **Services & Billing:** Fiber optic customers drive substantial revenue but also experience disproportionately high churn patterns. Similarly, electronic check users show elevated churn relative to other payment methods.
* **High Value vs. Risk:** High-value and medium-value tiers comprise the majority of retained customers. Notably, the **High Value–High Risk** segment contains **1,083 customers** contributing approximately **$2.81M in revenue**.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* 🎯 **Prioritize Retention:** Focus immediate retention strategies on the High Value–High Risk customer segment.
* 📝 **Encourage Long-Term Commitments:** Nudge month-to-month customers toward one-year or two-year contracts through targeted incentives.
* 🛡️ **Promote Bundled Services:** Create value packages including online security, device protection, and tech support to boost stickiness.
* 🔍 **Monitor Vulnerable Segments:** Pay close attention to senior citizens, fiber optic users, and electronic check customers to mitigate early churn signals.
